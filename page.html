#4471 · three problems, not one

owner support desk · living · reviewed 2026-11-10

#4471 was merged as one issue: "dates parse mm/dd in eu locales". Read by tenant, not by the intake tag, its 412 unique reports (the export has 427 rows; pages overlap) hold three different problems.

Not one bug reported many times. Fixing the parser does not close all of #4471.

The three problems

  1. Parser reads dates month first: 374 reports. Day-first tenants get day and month flipped. This is what #4471 describes. Engineering fixed it (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales).
  2. Booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY: 33 reports. Only the tenants on that form (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) are hit: their people type month first because the form tells them to. A different bug with a different fix. Engineering marked it won't fix ("booking-v3 form retired"), so those tenants stay broken until they move off the form.
  3. Dates move by one day: 5 reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620). "Invite arrived before I booked", "Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday". This is a time-zone or day-boundary problem. It is not a flip. The code files these under parser, and no one owns them.

374 + 33 + 5 = 412.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Merging by subject (decision 0001) never reads the body.
  • Intake's tag is a guess from the browser. It is wrong often: 15 of the 33 hint reports are tagged parser, and 23 parser reports are tagged hint.
  • The cause depends on the tenant's locale and booking form, which intake never looks up.

Lessons

  • Count by cause, not by subject. A big ticket can be several bugs.
  • A ticket marked known can still hold problems nobody owns. Re-read the bodies before you close it.
